Abstract 

In this work, we determine the generator polynomials for the Hermitian hulls and Hermitian sums of cyclic codes defined over the composite ring F2 × (F2 + vF2),where v2 = v. Based on these structures, we develop quantum error-correcting (QEC) codes by applying the Hermitian dual version of Quantum Construction X to the obtained Hermitian hulls and sums. Moreover, by employing matrix prod-uct code methods on linear complementary dual (LCD) codes defined over the same ring, we derive families of entanglement-assisted quantum error-correcting (EAQEC) codes. 

Keywords: Hermitian hulls of cyclic codes, Hermitian sums of cyclic codes, Quantum codes, Entanglement-assisted quantum codes, Quantum construction X 

MSC Classification: 94B05 , 94B15 

# 1 Introduction 

Quantum error correction (QEC) emerged alongside growing interest in quantum computers. In some research [1–5], it is suggested that quantum computers could out-perform classical ones on certain problems. The computational intractability of integer 1

> arXiv:2606.02137v1 [cs.IT] 1 Jun 2026

factorization constitutes the foundation of widely implemented public-key crypto-graphic schemes, such as RSA, which ensure the security of online communications and banking system. QEC techniques are essential for mitigating decoherence in quantum systems; without them, quantum computers would be limited to trivial problem sizes. Several constructions for these codes have been developed, among which the Calderbank-Shor-Steane (CSS) construction [6, 7] is particularly significant, as it derives a relationship between classical and quantum codes. In [8], quantum MDS codes are obtained from negacyclic codes. In [9], quantum BCH codes are constructed. In [10], some quantum and LCD codes are obtained. Hsieh et al. [11] introduced a foundational class of quantum codes, termed as entanglement-assisted quantum error-correcting codes (EAQECCs), which coherently integrate the theoretical benefits of entanglement-assisted and operator-based quan-tum error correction frameworks. In [12], EAQECCs are obtained by means of matrix-product codes. In [13], EAQECCs are constructed from algebraic geometry codes. One can look at the references [14–21]. In [22], QEC and EAQEC codes are obtained by means of Euclidean sums and hulls of cyclic codes over the ring F2 × (F2 + vF2). Inspired by [22], we will obtain QEC and EAQEC codes via Hermitian sums and hulls of cyclic code over the ring. Specifically, the codes we obtained in Theorem 10 using Hermitian sums and hulls are derived differently from the code parameters in Theorem 3.5 in [22]. This paper is organized as follows. In Section 2, we discuss the ring F2 ×(F2 +vF2), along with essential definitions and lemmas related to cyclic codes, the Gray map, Euclidean hulls and sums, and Hermitian hulls and sums. In Section 3, we examine the relationship between Hermitian hulls and sums of a linear code under the Gray map. By considering the framework of cyclic codes over F2 × (F2 + vF2), we determine the generators of the Hermitian hull and sum of a cyclic code over the ring. Then, we construct QEC codes from these Hermitian hulls and sums in Section 4. Thanks to Hermitian structure, we constructed new QEC codes that different from the codes obtained by Euclidean case. Furthermore, we obtain EAQEC codes through Quantum Construction X and matrix product codes in Section 5. The last section concludes the paper.

# 6 Conclusion 

In this study, we first obtain QEC codes from the Hermitian sums and hulls of cyclic codes over the ring F2 × (F2 + vF2), via the Gray map τ . Next, we examine the relationship between the Hermitian hulls of cyclic codes C over F2 × (F2 + vF2) and the Euclidean hulls of their Gray images τ (C), which are linear codes of length 3 n

over F2. This relationship is then used to get binary QEC codes via the Hermitian dual-based quantum construction X. Using Hermitian sums and hulls, we obtained new QEC code parameters according to the Euclidean case. Finally, we propose a method to construct EAQEC codes by utilizing matrix product codes of LCD codes over the ring F2 × (F2 + vF2). We believe that the theory presented in this work could 11 be generalized for primes p > 2, which might yield new and good parameters for both QECCs and EAQECCs.

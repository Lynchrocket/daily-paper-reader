## Yu Qian 1, Yu Wang 1,‡ , Liqi Wang 2

Abstract 

We establish the generator polynomials of the Euclidean and Hermitian duals of separable constacyclic codes over S = Fq × (Fq + vFq), with q an odd prime power and v2 = v, and we derive the generator polynomials of their Gray images, respectively. The generator poly-nomials of the Euclidean hulls and Hermitian hulls of separable constacyclic codes over S

and their Gray images are presented, respectively. Furthermore, we provide the generator polynomials of the Euclidean sums and Hermitian sums of separable constacyclic codes and their Gray images, respectively. Finally, we propose two methods to yield quantum error-correcting codes (QECCs) from the hulls and sums of separable constacyclic codes over S,and generate new QECCs that outperform the existing ones in terms of parameters. 

Keywords Hulls and sums, Separable constacyclic codes, Quantum construction X method. 

# 1 Introduction 

Quantum error correction is a key technology in quantum computing that protects quantum information from being disrupted by noise. Its core principle lies in using redundant qubits to store information and enhancing system stability through error detection and correction proce-dures. There are some famous methods to construct QECCs from classical error-correcting codes over finite field, such as: Calderbank–Shor–Steane (CSS) construction [1], Hermitian construc-tion [2] and quantum construction X [3]. In recent years, an increasing number of scholars have focused their attention on consta-cyclic codes over finite non-chain rings and utilized them to generate QECCs. In 2018, Gao et al. [4] employed u-constacyclic codes over Fp + uFp(u2 = 1) to obtain several novel QECCs by the CSS construction. Simultaneously, Ma et al. [5] characterized the basic framework of

20 5 Conclusion 

In this work, we put forward two methods to produce new QECCs from separable constacyclic codes over S = Fq × (Fq + vFq). First, we discussed the generator polynomials of the Eu-clidean and Hermitian hulls of separable S-t-constacyclic codes and their Gray images, where 

t = ( λ, β(1 − 2v)) . Then, the generator polynomials of the Euclidean and Hermitian sums and their Gray images were also presented. As an application, we derived lots of QECCs with im-proved parameters.
